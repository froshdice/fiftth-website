Before you bring one home

Good dog care starts with getting to know the dog.

Breed traits can be a helpful starting point, but every dog is an individual. A happy match is about the life you can share together.

✦ Breed is a clue, not a guarantee. Genetics, early experiences, health, and the individual dog's personality all shape who they become.
01 / TIME & ENERGY

Be honest about your everyday

Think about exercise, play, training, company, and the time you can reliably give—not just on your best days.

02 / HEALTH

Ask good health questions

Learn about breed-related health concerns. Ask a veterinarian or a responsible rescue or breeder for specific guidance.

03 / A KIND START

Choose responsible adoption

Meet the dog, ask about their needs and background, and avoid sellers who won't let you see where puppies are raised.

04 / YOUR DOG, YOUR BOND

Make room for an individual

Training with patience, enrichment, regular care, and positive social experiences help dogs feel safe and understood.
